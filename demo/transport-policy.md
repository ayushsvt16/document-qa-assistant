# Greenwood International School — Transport Policy 2024-2025

## Overview

The school provides transport facilities for students residing within a 15 km radius of the school campus. The transport service operates on fixed routes with designated pick-up and drop-off points.

## Bus Routes and Timings

### Morning Pick-up
- First pick-up: 7:00 AM
- Last pick-up: 7:45 AM
- School arrival: 8:00 AM

### Afternoon Drop-off
- Departure from school: 3:15 PM (Pre-Primary and Primary)
- Departure from school: 3:45 PM (Middle and Senior)
- All students dropped off by: 5:00 PM

## Transport Fee Structure

### Distance-Based Pricing
- Up to 5 km: ₹2,500 per month
- 5 km to 10 km: ₹3,500 per month
- 10 km to 15 km: ₹4,500 per month

### Payment Terms
- Transport fee is payable quarterly in advance.
- Quarter 1 (April-June): Due by March 31
- Quarter 2 (July-September): Due by June 30
- Quarter 3 (October-December): Due by September 30
- Quarter 4 (January-March): Due by December 31

## Safety Guidelines

1. All school buses are equipped with GPS tracking systems.
2. Each bus has a trained attendant in addition to the driver.
3. First aid kits are available on all buses.
4. Speed limit for school buses: 40 km/h within city limits.
5. Students must wear seat belts at all times during the journey.
6. CCTV cameras are installed in all school buses.

## Rules for Students

- Students must be at their designated bus stop 5 minutes before the scheduled time.
- The bus will not wait for late students beyond 2 minutes.
- Students must not stand while the bus is in motion.
- No eating or drinking on the bus except water.
- Students must carry their school ID card on the bus at all times.

## Route Changes and Cancellations

- A minimum of 15 days' notice is required for route changes.
- Transport service can be discontinued with one month's written notice.
- No refund for the ongoing quarter upon cancellation.
- Route changes are subject to seat availability.

## Liability

The school's transport service liability begins when the student boards the bus and ends when the student alights at the designated stop. Parents/guardians are responsible for the student's safety before boarding and after alighting.
