# Greenwood International School — Fee Structure 2024-2025

## Tuition Fees

The annual tuition fees for the academic year 2024-2025 are as follows:

### Pre-Primary Section
- Nursery: ₹45,000 per annum
- KG-1: ₹48,000 per annum
- KG-2: ₹48,000 per annum

### Primary Section
- Class 1 to Class 5: ₹55,000 per annum

### Middle Section
- Class 6 to Class 8: ₹65,000 per annum

### Senior Section
- Class 9 and Class 10: ₹75,000 per annum
- Class 11 and Class 12 (Science): ₹85,000 per annum
- Class 11 and Class 12 (Commerce): ₹78,000 per annum
- Class 11 and Class 12 (Humanities): ₹72,000 per annum

## Additional Fees

### One-Time Fees (at the time of admission)
- Admission Fee: ₹15,000 (non-refundable)
- Security Deposit: ₹10,000 (refundable upon leaving)
- Registration Fee: ₹2,000

### Annual Fees
- Laboratory Fee (Classes 6-12): ₹5,000
- Library Fee: ₹2,000
- Technology Fee: ₹3,500
- Sports and Activities Fee: ₹4,000
- Examination Fee: ₹3,000

## Payment Schedule

Fees can be paid in the following installments:
1. First Installment: Before April 15 (50% of annual tuition + all annual fees)
2. Second Installment: Before October 15 (remaining 50% of annual tuition)

## Late Payment Policy

- A late fee of ₹500 per month will be charged after the due date.
- If fees remain unpaid for more than 3 months, the student may not be allowed to attend classes.
- A grace period of 7 days is provided after the due date before the late fee is applied.

## Fee Concessions

- Sibling Discount: 10% concession on tuition fee for the second child enrolled in the school.
- Merit Scholarship: Up to 25% concession for students securing above 95% in the previous year's final examination.
- Staff Ward: 50% concession on tuition fee for children of school employees.

## Refund Policy

- Full refund of security deposit upon withdrawal (after adjusting any outstanding dues).
- No refund of admission fee or registration fee.
- Pro-rata refund of tuition fee if withdrawal is within the first month of the academic year.
- No tuition fee refund after the first month.

For any fee-related queries, please contact the accounts office at accounts@greenwoodschool.edu.
