# Greenwood International School — Examination Policy 2024-2025

## Assessment Structure

The school follows a Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) pattern with the following assessment distribution:

### Term 1 (April - September)
- Unit Test 1 (July): 20 marks - Weightage: 10%
- Mid-Term Examination (September): 80 marks - Weightage: 30%
- Internal Assessment: 20 marks - Weightage: 10%
  - Projects: 5 marks
  - Assignments: 5 marks
  - Class Participation: 5 marks
  - Attendance: 5 marks

### Term 2 (October - March)
- Unit Test 2 (December): 20 marks - Weightage: 10%
- Final Examination (March): 80 marks - Weightage: 30%
- Internal Assessment: 20 marks - Weightage: 10%

## Examination Rules

1. Students must arrive 15 minutes before the examination starts.
2. No electronic devices (mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators unless permitted) allowed in the examination hall.
3. Students must carry their school ID card and admit card to every examination.
4. No student will be allowed to enter the examination hall 30 minutes after the exam has started.
5. No student is allowed to leave the examination hall during the first hour.
6. Use of unfair means will result in immediate cancellation of the paper and may lead to suspension.

## Re-examination Policy

- Students who miss an examination due to medical reasons may apply for a re-examination.
- A valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted within 3 days of the examination date.
- Re-examination fee: ₹500 per subject.
- Maximum 2 subjects can be re-examined per term.
- Re-examination marks are capped at 90% of the maximum marks.

## Promotion Criteria

### Classes 1-8
- Minimum 33% aggregate marks required for promotion.
- A student failing in more than 2 subjects will be detained.
- Students will be given one chance for re-examination in failed subjects.

### Classes 9-10
- As per CBSE Board guidelines.
- Minimum 33% in each subject required.

### Classes 11-12
- As per CBSE Board guidelines.
- Minimum attendance of 75% required to appear for Board examinations.

## Academic Integrity

The school has a zero-tolerance policy towards academic dishonesty, including:
- Copying from another student's answer sheet
- Bringing unauthorized materials into the examination hall
- Sharing answers or question papers during or after the examination
- Plagiarism in assignments and projects

Penalties range from zero marks in the specific examination to suspension from school, depending on the severity of the offense.

## Report Cards

- Report cards are issued twice a year (after each term examination).
- Parent-teacher meetings are scheduled within two weeks of report card distribution.
- Digital copies of report cards are available on the school portal.
